items from Benzinga** on this pass, as it did on 2026-09-17. It is one outlet
and cannot corroborate itself. Both items below were therefore taken to SEC

Company's own words, from the exhibit: 22% placebo-adjusted weight loss at
week 33 on weekly 17.5 mg, "no plateau observed"; up to 97% of weight loss
maintained on every-other-week dosing for three months and up to 90% on
monthly; GI adverse-event rates "similar to placebo". Conference call 08:00 ET
today. **Mechanism:** a maintenance-dosing result widens the addressable
duration of therapy and is the adherence objection the whole GLP-1 category
carries, so it re-prices the franchise rather than one trial. VKTX +34.4% on
the day (30.11 -> 40.46, consolidated).
