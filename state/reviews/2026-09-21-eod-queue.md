## 2. The live entry is a pullback; the study measured a chase

**Severity: medium, but it undermines published evidence.** The order sits
2.2% *below* the market as a limit. `docs/15-point-in-time-study.md` modelled
entry at the **next open** — i.e. filling on the gap. Those are different
strategies:

- the study's version chases the gap and always fills
- the live version refuses to chase and fills only on a pullback that may
  never come

Today is the demonstration: a signal fired, an order went out, and it has not
filled because the market gapped away. A backtest that assumes the fill
overstates participation and measures a strategy we do not run. The study's
result was already not statistically significant; this weakens it further.

To decide at EOD: which behaviour we actually want, then make the study match
the live path (or the live path match the study). Do not change either today.
