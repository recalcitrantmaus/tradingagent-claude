## The guardrail that fired, and the one that did not

**Fired, and mattered for the first time:** the empty-plan binding. Friday's
plan was `[]`, and verified read-only, the mechanical scanner *would* have
submitted FPS and MARA. Both previous empty plans this week were the
coincidence where the scanner happened to agree. **This is the first time a
written refusal actually overrode a live signal** — which is exactly the defect
found in Wednesday's rehearsal, now demonstrably fixed.

**Did not fire, and should have:** reconciliation cannot see equity drift.
`tools/trading_day.py:48` compares ledger position symbols against broker
position symbols plus `trading_blocked`, then adopts the broker's equity
wholesale. It never checks equity against what the ledger expects. Equity moved
$300.00 → $299.99 with no position change, and every check returned clean:
`trading_day open` printed `$299.99` and `0 closed trades` in one breath, the
close reported **"Escalate to the human today? — nothing"**, and `tools.review`
still says "no closed trades".

At one cent this is cosmetic. The mechanism is not. An unrecorded fill, a fee,
a position opened and closed between two firings, or a bug in the submit path
all present identically — equity moves, positions match, reconciliation returns
`ok` — while the drawdown lock and the −4%/−8% halts keep measuring against a
ledger that has drifted. **This is the most important thing in this report.**

## What a reviewer should look at — judgement deliberately left open

`python3 -m tools.model_check weekly-review` **exits 1** in this session
(Sonnet tier; weekly review needs Opus). No rule was promoted, changed or
deleted, and no threshold was touched. The following are for a session cleared
to decide them:

1. **Fix reconciliation to compare equity, not just positions.** Highest
   priority. It touches the path every halt depends on, so it needs a test and
   a lessons entry. A cent is noise; a dollar at this size is not.
2. **Decide whether the structure-based 1.5R bar is right for this universe.**
   Friday is one data point against it and it is a weak one, but it is the
   second signal this direction. This wants a backtest of structure-based R
   across the committed universe — not another discretionary week.
3. **Decide whether the mechanical scanner should be able to veto on R at all.**
   Today its R is a constant. With the empty plan as the only real control, that
   is a single point of failure.
4. **Decide what a $300 account does about scale-outs.** Friday's engine sizes
   were 1, 3 and 5 shares. "Sell half at the first target, trail the rest" is
   impossible at 1 share and coarse at 3, so the live strategy is structurally
   cruder than the simulated one and will stay that way until equity is several
   times larger.
5. **Rebuild the Routines in the claude.ai UI for week two.** Per `HANDOFF.md`:
   fresh session per firing, notifications, and the MCP connectors Phase 3
   needs. This week's per-day persistent sessions do not scale to five days.
