# Friday 2026-09-25 and Phase 2 week two, in plain language

## Today

The account did not trade today. The 09:45 ET morning session was blocked before it could start. The block came
from Claude Code's own permission check for unattended sessions, not from the trading rules. That check refused to
run the command that reconciles the account against the broker (finding F-1). Because the morning found nothing
to do, the midday and 15:30 sessions were cancelled, as the standing rule says.

At 11:45 ET, a separate test ran the same commands one at a time, in the exact form the permission allowlist
expects. **Every command ran.** The 16:05 ET close then ran its full sequence the same way, and nothing was refused.
The fix for F-1 therefore works for commands written the way the allowlist expects.

At the close, the account held no positions and no orders, and nothing was unprotected over the weekend. The
account's cash matched the broker to the cent.

The week's only trade was DT on Wednesday. It was bought at $58.47 and sold at $58.10. The stop did not
cause the sale. The system misread the broker's normal "held" order status as a failure and flattened the position.
That mistake has been fixed and is covered by tests (`state/journal/2026-09-23.md`).

**Be honest about what this shows.** The +5% target is a hurdle rate, not a quota, and the rules forbid closing the
gap with leverage. Missing it by 5.12 points in a week with one small trade is expected. But a nearly flat account
does **not** show that capital preservation works. It is an absence of evidence. One trade is far too small a
sample to say anything about the strategy, whether good or bad. Most of this week's output was infrastructure:
tests, controls, and the unattended-session plumbing. Infrastructure is not the exit criterion.
