## RKLB — REJECTED: the signal does not exist on the consolidated tape

The screen reported `RKLB 70.76 over 70.56 (+0.28%)`. On consolidated (SIP)
data the 20-day high excluding today is **71.21**, and RKLB last traded
**71.05** — *below* the level. There is no breakout.

```
         20d high (excl today)      last     breakout?
IEX               70.56            70.95        YES
consolidated      71.21            71.05         no
```

`adapters/alpaca.py:177` pins `feed=iex` on every bar request. IEX reports a
subset of prints, so its daily high can only be **at or below** the
consolidated high — the error is one-directional and it manufactures
breakouts, never hides them. Measured across 40 universe names this morning:
IEX's 20-day high is below consolidated in **29 of 40 (72%)**, median
understatement 0.02%, **max 0.91%** — and RKLB's gap is 0.91%, the tail of
that distribution landing exactly on today's signal. **1 phantom breakout, 0
missed breakouts**, which is the asymmetry the mechanism predicts.

Recorded as **M-30**. Not a trade, and the analyst headline circulating on
RKLB today is unrelated to why it screened.
