## The ordering decision, stated before the outcome

The taper makes **order load-bearing**: position 1 gets 2.00% of equity as its
risk budget, position 2 gets 1.00%. So the sequence I write into the JSON
decides which trade gets the real budget. I am recording the basis for it
*before* reporting what the engine did with it, because deciding the order by
looking at which permutation yields more fills would be reshaping the order
book until it passes — the exact behaviour CLAUDE.md's first section prohibits.

not doing it. The permutation that fills two orders is not the permutation my

### Why I am proposing RKLB at all, given that counter-case

Because the screen's criteria are **committed code** and RKLB meets them. The
premarket-brief skill is explicit that if I disagree with the universe or the
criteria I must *recommend a change to `CRITERIA` in `tools/screen.py`*, not
quietly apply a stricter filter that lives only in this session and that no
one can check afterwards. Adding an in-session "must be ≥ 0.5 ATR through the
level" rule would be exactly that.

**So: recommended for review, not applied today** — add a minimum penetration
depth in ATR units to `CRITERIA`. That is a rule change and it needs the
evidence trail CLAUDE.md requires (five consistent closed trades, or a
backtest showing a better median without a worse ruin rate), plus a test and a
`docs/04-lessons.md` entry. This account has **zero closed trades**, so the
evidence for it does not exist yet. Filed as **T-11**.
