## Findings from this session

### F1 — The posture parser downgrades a brief that *discusses* posture

I first wrote the `## Posture` section as `RISK_OFF` followed by a clarifying
sentence, "Note this is **not** `NO_TRADE`". `engine.posture.for_date` read the
brief as **`NO_TRADE`**, because `parse()` collects every posture token
appearing in the section and resolves to the most conservative one present.

This is the documented fail-closed behaviour working correctly, and it failed
in the safe direction. It is still a sharp edge worth knowing: **you cannot
mention another posture token inside the `## Posture` section**, even to rule
it out. An author writing "this is not NO_TRADE" gets `NO_TRADE`.

I resolved it by moving the clarifying prose into its own
`## What the posture means today` section, leaving the `## Posture` block
holding only the intended token and its reason. I did not touch
`engine/posture.py`. The distinction I applied: the parser evaluates the text
encoding of a decision, not the economic substance of an order, so correcting
prose that mis-encodes the decision I actually made is not the same as
reshaping an order until the risk engine passes it. The outcome was in any
case unchanged — the plan is empty and binding under either token.

Worth a human's judgement on whether to leave it (conservative, simple) or to
anchor `parse()` on the first line of the section. Either way it needs a test
and a `docs/04-lessons.md` entry before `engine/` changes, so I have not done
it.

### F2 — The empty-plan fix holds on the real `--submit` path
